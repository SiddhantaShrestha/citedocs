Northline Studio bonus review for 2026. Admins only. The employee handbook tells the team that bonuses are decided in the spring. It does not give the pool, the payment day, or the names deadline. Do not paste those details into the handbook or into #general.

Managers draft names in the second week of March. That meeting is not on the team calendar. Bring last year's notes, not the employee handbook. The handbook only says that leadership decides bonuses in the spring, with no pool and no date.

The 2026 engineering bonus pool is $48,000. Bonuses are paid on the last Friday of April, which is 24 April 2026. Managers send proposed names and amounts to Lena Ortiz by 10 April 2026. Use the sheet titled "Eng bonus 2026" in the admin drive. After Lena signs the sheet, payroll locks the file.

No single engineering bonus in this cycle may exceed $8,000. Amounts are gross, before tax. The pool is not a promise that every engineer receives a payment. A manager may recommend zero with a one-line reason.

Payroll sends each person a letter in the week after 24 April 2026. The letter states that person's amount and no one else's. The office calendar does not show 24 April as a bonus date. The team expense policy is not used to pay these bonuses.

Direct questions to lena.ortiz@northline.studio. Do not discuss another person's amount.
