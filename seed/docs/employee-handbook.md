Northline Studio employee handbook for the Demo team. This file covers the Portland office, the working day, and who to ask. It does not list salaries, bonus amounts, or staffing changes. Those notes are kept for admins.

The studio sits on the 4th floor of 18 Harbor Lane, Portland. Jonah Hale staffs reception from 8:30 to 17:30 on weekdays. Visitors sign the paper log and wait in the lobby until their host comes down. The door code is not written in this handbook.

Working hours are 9:00 to 17:30. Core hours, when you should be reachable on Slack, are 10:00 to 16:00 Pacific. Full-time staff may work from home on two days each week. Monday is an office day for the whole team, so do not choose Monday as a home day. New hires work from the office for their first two weeks.

Annual bonuses, if any, are decided by leadership in the spring. This handbook does not list amounts, payment dates, or eligibility. Ask your manager. Do not look for a dollar figure here. Pay bands are also outside this file.

The shared kitchen is on the north side of the 4th floor. Put your name on food you store. The fridge is emptied every Friday at 16:00, and unlabeled containers are thrown away that afternoon. Maya Chen posts the day's visitor list each morning. If you are hosting a guest, email Maya before 9:00 so reception can print a badge. Guests do not get a door code.

Time off, including the number of annual leave days, is in the leave policy. Spending rules, including the $250 limit a manager can approve, are in the expense policy. Lost laptops and account rules are in the security policy. Your first week is described in the onboarding guide.

Probation lasts 90 days from your start date. Your manager meets you on day 30 and day 60. Company laptops stay company property when you leave. Questions about this handbook go to people@northline.studio.
