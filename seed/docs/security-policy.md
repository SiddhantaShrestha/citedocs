Northline Studio security policy for everyone on the Demo team. It covers accounts, laptops, and the office network. It does not describe who is hired or how teams are organized.

Store passwords in the studio 1Password vault. Do not keep them in Slack, in a notes app, or in a browser profile on a personal computer. Turn on the vault's device unlock. Shared logins live in the vault folder named Studio, not in a direct message.

Every company laptop uses FileVault or BitLocker. IT checks encryption on the first Monday of each month. You cannot turn disk encryption off to install a tool. Ask IT in the Slack channel #it-help and wait for the ticket.

The screen locks after 5 minutes away from the desk. Do not share your badge, and do not hold the 4th floor door for someone who forgot theirs. Jonah Hale can print a temporary badge at reception after he checks the staff list.

Report a lost or stolen laptop to security@northline.studio within 1 hour of noticing it is gone, including nights and weekends. Include the serial number from your onboarding card if you still have it. IT will remote-lock the machine. Do not wait until the next office day. A guest must not be left alone with an unlocked laptop.

USB storage drives are blocked on company laptops. Do not copy client files onto a personal stick. Use the Studio Drive folder instead. The guest wifi password changes on the first Monday of each month. Jonah Hale prints the new password and leaves it at reception. Do not send the guest wifi password by email to someone outside the studio.

Forward suspicious mail to security@northline.studio. Do not click the link to "check what it does." Questions about access go to the same address.
