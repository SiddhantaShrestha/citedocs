Northline Studio expense policy for the Demo team. Use the company card when you have one. If you pay yourself, submit the expense before you expect repayment. This policy is the team copy. It does not describe salary bands.

File expenses in Expensify within 14 days of the purchase. A manager can approve a claim up to $250. Claims above that amount wait for finance. The employee handbook mentions this same $250 manager limit and does not add a higher cap.

The studio pays for client travel, client meals, and tools you need for a client project. Software under the manager limit can go on the company card. Personal subscriptions, the commute to 18 Harbor Lane, and gifts for coworkers are not reimbursed.

A meal on the company card needs no receipt when the total is under $40. Keep the receipt when the meal is $40 or more. Client dinners over $75 per person need approval from Priya Shah in finance before you book the table. Email priya.shah@northline.studio with the guest list and the estimated total. A dinner booked without that approval is not reimbursed.

Mileage for a personal car used on studio business is $0.67 per mile. Record the start and end address in Expensify. Commuting from home to 18 Harbor Lane is not reimbursed.

The hotel cap is $220 per night in Portland and $310 per night in New York. Book through the Egencia portal. A rate above the cap needs Priya Shah's written approval before check-in. Internet in the hotel is reimbursed. Minibar charges and in-room movies are not.

Attach a photo of each required receipt. Claims missing a receipt after 14 days are sent back. Questions go to finance@northline.studio.
