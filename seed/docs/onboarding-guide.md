Northline Studio onboarding guide for a new hire on the Demo team. Read this before your first Monday. Pay figures are not in this guide. Your offer letter is the only place those numbers appear for you.

Arrive at 9:30 on your first day. Jonah Hale meets you at reception on the 4th floor of 18 Harbor Lane. Bring a photo ID. You will get a badge before you go upstairs. The employee handbook explains the 9:00 to 17:30 day and the Monday office rule. Follow those from week one.

Your laptop is requested on the IT queue named new-hire. The ticket is opened when your start date is confirmed. If the laptop is not on your desk at 9:30, tell Jonah and he will ping #it-help. Do not use a personal laptop for client files while you wait.

In your first hour, join the Slack channels #general and #team-demo. Your manager adds you to the project channel for your pod. Benefits enrollment closes on day 30. After that date you wait until the next open enrollment in November. The form is in the email from people@northline.studio with the subject "Enroll by day 30."

The buddy for engineering hires who start in 2026 is Samir Okonkwo. Samir meets you for lunch on day 1 and again on day 10. He does not approve leave or expenses. Those still go to your manager. The leave policy states the 22 day balance and the 10 business day notice. The security policy states that a lost laptop is reported within 1 hour.

Probation is 90 days, with check-ins on day 30 and day 60, as the handbook says. Questions about your first week go to people@northline.studio.
