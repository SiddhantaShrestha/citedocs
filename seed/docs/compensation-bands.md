Northline Studio compensation bands. Admins only. Do not copy these ranges into the employee handbook, the onboarding guide, or a team Slack channel. A candidate sees only the number in their own offer letter.

Read this note next to the bonus review. The team expense policy already says a manager can approve $250. This file is where the higher admin limit is written, and it is the only copy of the salary ranges. Offers are drafted by the hiring manager and checked by people operations before they are sent. Ranges from 2025 are void. Do not reuse an old spreadsheet.

Bands below are annual base salary for Portland. The location adjustment for Portland is 0%. A hire in New York uses the same band plus 12%. Offers are quoted as base salary. Equity is listed separately and is not included in the band.

The engineering IC2 band is $118,000 to $146,000. The engineering IC3 band is $148,000 to $178,000. A new IC2 offer should start at or below the midpoint of $132,000 unless the hiring manager writes a reason. An offer above the midpoint of either band needs written approval from Lena Ortiz, VP People, before the letter is sent.

IC2 equity is 4,000 options. IC3 equity is 7,500 options. Vesting is 4 years with a 1 year cliff. The option plan is the 2024 plan. Do not promise a refresh in the offer letter.

Managers may approve expenses up to $250, which is the same cap written in the team expense policy. Spend from $251 to $2,000 needs Lena Ortiz. Spend above $2,000 needs both Lena Ortiz and Priya Shah. That higher cap is not in the team expense policy.

Bonus amounts are not in this note. See the bonus review for the 2026 pool and the payment date. Questions about a live offer go to lena.ortiz@northline.studio.
