Northline Studio leave policy for full-time employees on the Demo team. Contractors follow their statement of work, not this page. The employee handbook points here for day counts and does not repeat them.

Request annual leave in Workday at least 10 business days before the first day you want off. Your manager approves or declines within 3 business days. The request is not final until Workday shows Approved. Do not book flights before that status.

Full-time staff receive 22 days of annual leave in each calendar year, plus the Oregon public holidays the studio closes for. You may carry at most 5 unused days into the next year. Those carried days expire on 31 March. They are not paid out when you leave, except where state law says otherwise.

Sick leave is a separate balance. You have 8 sick days each year. Message your manager before 9:30 on the morning you are out. If you are sick for 3 or more working days in a row, send a doctor's note. The studio accepts a note from Dr. Patel at Harbor Lane Clinic or from any licensed GP. Do not post the diagnosis in Slack. Email the note to people@northline.studio.

The primary caregiver may take 16 weeks of parental leave. That leave can start no earlier than 4 weeks before the due date or the placement date. Tell people@northline.studio at least 8 weeks before the start, unless the date was not known that early. A one-day sick absence does not change bonus timing. A parental leave that covers the April payroll run is handled by payroll, not by this policy.

When you return, confirm your hours in Workday on the Friday of that week. Questions about balances go to people@northline.studio.
